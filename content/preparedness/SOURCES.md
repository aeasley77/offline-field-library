## Limitations

The CDC handout is dated August 2021. Guidance, local services, evacuation routes, and emergency contacts can change. This library is a reference aid and does not replace current instructions from local emergency management, public health officials, or emergency responders. For a life-threatening emergency, call 911 when available.
